# Research

Investigate against the source that owns the fact: official documentation, specifications, source code, first-party APIs, release notes or primary data. Use community sources for experience and discovery, not as silent substitutes for primary evidence.

Separate claims from interpretation. Capture citations or artifact references with every material claim. When sources conflict, preserve the conflict until discriminating evidence resolves it. Keep unresolved points `UNKNOWN`.

Parallelize independent source hunts when useful, but synthesize once through the governor. Research output is evidence for a decision, not an implementation mandate.
